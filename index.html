Our Story

About Malu Ixel

Welcome to Malu Ixel Coffee House — where Latin roots meet fresh flavors. Located in the heart of Magna, Utah, we're a cozy cafe dedicated to serving exceptional coffee, homemade Latin-inspired dishes, and warm hospitality.

From our handcrafted espresso drinks to our chilaquiles and tres leches cake, every item is made with love and authenticity. Come for the coffee, stay for the community.
